An innovative and interactive platform for connecting teachers and student the Holy Qur'an. The app offers features for students, teachers, and supervisors, enabling collaborative and guided Qur'an study. Users can listen to the complete Qur'an, receive feedback on memorization, and manage student-teacher interactions. Available on [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/maqraa-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A3%D8%A9/id6686394956). My work involved designing and developing the app from scratch, implementing a robust backend, and ensuring a seamless and reliable user experience.
